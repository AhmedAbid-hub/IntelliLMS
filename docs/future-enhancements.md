# Future enhancements

Immediate continuation priorities are in limitations.md. After those are complete:

- Institution-specific model registries, approved pre-outcome feature snapshots, drift detection and calibrated external evaluation.
- Additional reporting dimensions and sustained-load optimization beyond the implemented scoped comparisons and recorded-event trends.
- Scalable media storage with scanning, lifecycle management and signed authorization-aware downloads.
- Rich text authoring with a vetted sanitizer, drag-and-drop ordering and accessible calendar views.
- Immutable grading-policy versions, official finalized transcripts and rubric grading; provisional GPA and configurable repeat-attempt selection are already implemented.
- Additional authentication policies such as institution-managed SSO and MFA after a separate threat/design review.
- Broader screen-reader/cross-browser certification and localization; configured institute terminology and keyboard-capable workflows are already implemented.

These are future work, not hidden mock implementations or promises of deployed behavior.
